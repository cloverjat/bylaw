How it works

1

Pick a rule

Like "everyone pays, then one person gets the pot, in turn." You read it in plain words.

2

We prove it

The rule is checked against every possible case, so it can't hide a trick.

3

Locked for good

It goes on X Layer. After that nobody can change it, and the money follows the rule automatically.

What these words mean
Rule
A small program on the blockchain that decides when money can move. You read it in plain words, and nobody can change it after it is locked.
Circle
A group saving plan. Everyone pays the same amount each round, and the whole pot goes to one member per round, in turn.
Payroll
Pay for a team, released automatically at the end of each cycle by a public rule.
Step
The unit a wallet limit is counted in. If one step is 5 USDC, a limit of 10 steps means 50 USDC.
Proof
A check that the rule behaves correctly for every possible case before it is locked.
USDC
A digital dollar. One USDC is worth one US dollar.
X Layer
The blockchain where Bylaw rules live. It is run by OKX.
Gas (OKB)
A small network fee, paid in OKB, to record something on X Layer.
Wallet address
Your public account number. It starts with 0x. Anyone can send money to it, but only you can spend from it.
